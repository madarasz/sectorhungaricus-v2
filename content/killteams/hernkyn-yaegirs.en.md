Hernkyn Yaegirs are the hardened commandos of the Leagues of Votann, sent ahead to infiltrate new worlds and guard their kindreds' resource claims. They are a slow but tough squad of scouts, with a specialist for each job, from sniper and missile gunner to mine-layer and knife fighter, and Warriors to fill the ranks. To make up for their lack of speed, they can move forward out of their drop zone at the start of the battle. They carry supplies for any situation, so while no enemy is close, they can push themselves to act more or patch up their wounds.

Murderwings are packs of sadistic chaos space marine Raptors who terrify their prey before they tear it apart. They are a mobile, elite melee team, but they are not very durable. All of them can fly. Their jump packs let them leap over terrain and other operatives, ambush enemies from behind buildings and choose the fights that suit them.

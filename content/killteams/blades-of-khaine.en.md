Athletic, graceful and utterly lethal, the Blades of Khaine are Aeldari Aspect Warriors sent to cut the strands of fate before foreseen disasters can happen. The team mixes warriors from three Aspect shrines: Howling Banshees cross the battlefield over any obstacle, Striking Scorpions strike from hiding, and Dire Avengers are sharpshooters who can shoot on the move. Each shrine has five combat techniques of its own that work like ploys. They make the team very effective, but they are a lot for both players to keep track of. The team is fast and hits hard, but it is fragile for an elite team.

To the Adeptus Mechanicus, gathering knowledge is holy work, and they send their cybernetically altered Skitarii and Sicarians to do it at any cost. The team has a flexible roster that mixes long-range Skitarii Rangers, irradiated Skitarii Vanguard and Sicarian assassins. Each turning point, their Tech-Priest master sends a new doctrine that improves one part of the team, such as shooting, melee, speed or toughness, and usually weakens another.

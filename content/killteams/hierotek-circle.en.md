Led by ambitious, scheming Crypteks, Hierotek Circles use necron soldiers to sabotage, rob and murder the rivals of their dynasty. In this ranged necron team, a Cryptek leads slow but very tough Immortals and Deathmarks, with two small Plasmacyte constructs. The first time an operative is incapacitated, it can rise again and rejoin the fight. All operatives heal a small amount each turn.

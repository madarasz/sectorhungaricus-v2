Wrecka Krews are ork gangs obsessed with destruction, who blow up bunkers and battle tanks with rokkits and hammers. This aggressive elite ork team mixes ranged Tankbustas, Breaka Boyz with brutal melee weapons and expendable Bomb Squigs. Their best attack rolls build momentum that they can spend to turn missed attacks into hits.

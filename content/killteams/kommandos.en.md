Unlike most orks, Kommandos like dirty tricks and a quiet approach, but once the fighting starts their love of violence takes over. This ork team mixes Boys led by a Boss Nob with wide variety of one-of-a-kind specialists. Their faction rule lets them charge even while concealed, so they stay hidden for close combat.

Shaped by the Hive Mind, Raveners burrow at horrific speed and strike their prey from vulnerable angles with talons, claws and biotoxins. They are an elite tyranid melee team of only five operatives. They cannot survive focused fire, and losing even one operative hurts them badly. During the battle they dig a tunnel, and they can come up anywhere along it to strike, then go back underground to safety.

Vespids are a highly aggressive, winged xenos species that fight for the t’au, who keep them in check with communion helms. They are an offensive t’au team with excellent mobility, because they can fly over terrain and other operatives. They are not accurate shooters, but lucky hits cause heavy damage, some shots keep hurting the target later. Their guns charge up and pierce armour better if they are on the move. By instinct they shoot and charge the closest enemy and ignore objectives, unless you spend Communion points to control them.

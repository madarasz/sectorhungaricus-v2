Legionaries are bitter veterans with millenia of combat experience. Some are savage killers, and others are devoted servants of the Dark Gods. They are an elite chaos space marine team of tough operatives, with a roster that mixes melee specialists and gunners. Each operative takes the mark of one of the Dark Gods, which gives it its own gift, for example harder melee attacks or protection from damage.

Spectre Squads are the elite scouts of the Astra Militarum, and they fight their wars with sabotage and ambush. This ranged control team has fragile stats and little melee strength, and it plays by limiting the opponent's options and forcing bad moves. Their Elite Fieldcraft rule lets them react in the middle of an enemy activation: a ready Spectre can shoot the enemy that has just acted, or slip away to safety.

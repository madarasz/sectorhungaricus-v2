Tempestus Aquilons are among the Imperium's most highly trained soldiers, and they drop into battle on grav-chutes, wearing carapace armour and carrying hot-shot lascarbines. Most of the team starts the game above the killzone and lands later near its drop markers with Drop Insertion. This keeps them safe from early attacks and lets them land in better positions.

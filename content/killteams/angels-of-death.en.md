Genetically modified transhuman warriors, space marines are amongst Humanity’s most elite fighting forces. This is a generic space marine team from the Imperium. They have various chapter tactic options, a flexible roster that can be suited to both melee and ranged combat. Their sturdy operatives tolerate mistakes, and their simple rules make them one of the best beginner-friendly team.

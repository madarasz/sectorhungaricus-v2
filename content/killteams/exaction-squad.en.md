Exaction Squads are the most skilled Arbitrators of the Adeptus Arbites, sent to hunt down the worst criminals of the Imperium and bring them back alive for judgement. They are a human team that mixes shotgun-armed enforcers with shield-and-maul brawlers, heavy gunners and a cyber-mastiff. Their ruthless efficiency lets them shoot at enemies that are locked in melee.

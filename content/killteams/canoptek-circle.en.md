Emotionless Canoptek constructs tear through rock, metal and living flesh for their Cryptek master, who taps ancient nodes of power to charge them further. This necron team has eight operatives: the Geomancer; tough Tomb Crawlers with powerful guns and fast, expendable Macrocytes that keep returning to the fight. The obelisk nodes link up into a matrix of power, and operatives inside it hit more reliably and can do more actions in each activation.

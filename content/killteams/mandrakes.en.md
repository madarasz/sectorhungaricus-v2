Once Drukhari themselves, Mandrakes are creatures of living darkness feared even by their own kin, able to emerge from any foe’s shadow to strike with frigid claws before melting back into the cursed realm they call home. Their operatives are fragile, but all are armed with glimmersteel blades and soul-freezing baleblasts that ignore enemy armor. A Mandrake standing in shadow can vanish and reappear in another part of the killzone that is not visible to its enemies.

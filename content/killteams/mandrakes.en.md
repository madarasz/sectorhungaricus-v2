Mandrakes come from a realm beyond reality and often fight as mercenaries for the Drukhari, slipping past their foes unseen thanks to their mastery of shadows. Their operatives are fragile, but all are armed with glimmersteel blades and soul-freezing baleblasts that ignore enemy armor. A Mandrake standing in shadow can vanish and reappear in another part of the killzone that is not visible to its enemies.

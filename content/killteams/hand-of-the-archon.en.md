Drukhari Archons who need a subtle touch for their schemes send Hand of the Archon teams, bands of assassins, thieves and torturers with exotic and cruel weapons. These fast but fragile aeldari carry rifles, pistols and blades, so they can both shoot and fight. They feed on pain: whenever they injure or kill an enemy, they gain power that lets them take extra actions, heal or move again after a kill.

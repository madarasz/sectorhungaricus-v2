Wherever discontent festers, people turn to the Dark Gods, and the most fanatical of them are warped into monsters with claws and leathery wings. This Chaos horde team has 14 fragile, melee-focused operatives, mostly cultists with crude weapons and pistols. During the battle, Devotees turn into Mutants and Mutants into daemon-possessed Torments, so the cult becomes more dangerous as the fight goes on.

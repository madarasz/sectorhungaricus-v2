Cold and calculating, Battleclades are Adeptus Mechanicus hunting parties sent to take apart the enemies of the Omnissiah and claim lost technology. Tech-Priests lead a crew of slow servitors armed with heavy guns, flamers and repair tools. The servitors are weak in melee. They share a network, so one servitor can pass its power to another. This lets them act at unexpected moments, even after they have activated.

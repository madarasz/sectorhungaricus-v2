Cold and calculating, Battleclades are Adeptus Mechanicus hunting parties sent to take apart the enemies of the Omnissiah and claim lost technology. Tech-Priests lead a crew of slow servitors armed with heavy guns and flamers. The servitors are weak in melee. Their Noospheric network allows one servitor to pass its processing power to another. This lets them act at unexpected moments, even after they have activated.

Goremongers are devotees of Khorne who copy the bladework and military discipline of Bloodletter daemons. They are a fast chaos melee team. Khorne's runes carved into their flesh limit the damage that one shooting attack can do to them. Each operative carries a tank of blood-infused stimms, which it refills by killing enemies. It can be spent to heal, move further or hit harder.

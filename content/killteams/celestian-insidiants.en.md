Veteran Celestians of the Adepta Sororitas, the Insidiants hunt witches and heretics and long to die as martyrs. They are a small, elite team of tough Sisters of Battle who fight mostly in melee and carry anti-psyker weapons and equipment. When an inspired sister falls, a nearby sister gains a lasting blessing that lets her act more, heal or perform a Dash.

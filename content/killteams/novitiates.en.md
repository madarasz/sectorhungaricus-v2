Sisters Novitiate are young recruits of the Adepta Sororitas who hunt apostates and heretics and hope to one day become full Battle Sisters. They are a lightly armoured team that fights mostly in melee, and almost every operative is a different specialist. When they shoot, fight or are shot at, they can spend faith to re-roll a die or improve a result.

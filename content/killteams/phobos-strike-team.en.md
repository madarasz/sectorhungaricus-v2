Phobos Strike Teams are stealthy space marine specialists who carry explosives, comms gear and precise weapons, and they can outmanoeuvre enemy forces much larger than their own. This elite Imperium team mixes Infiltrators, Incursors and Reivers. Infiltrators carry omni-scramblers that jam enemy communications, so one chosen enemy operative cannot activate until other enemy operatives have acted. Reivers terrify their victims, so enemy operatives near them find it harder to perform mission actions. Incursors carry sensor arrays, and enemy operatives cannot be obscured when they shoot at them.

Savage and sadistic in the extreme, the Night Lords use fear itself as their most potent weapon. They are a close-combat-focused space marine team with several tricks to disrupt the opponent's plan. They excel against other space marine teams. Hiding in the shadows, they become obscured outside 8" if they are near heavy cover or under vantage.
